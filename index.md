# Breatho Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 26 August 2026**

Breatho is a guided breathing app. It is built to work without an account and
without collecting personal information. This policy explains exactly what that
means.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Breatho does not collect, transmit, or store any personal
information on our servers. We do not operate a server that receives your data.

There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, and no advertising identifier.

## What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone and never leaves it:

- Your completed breathing sessions (pattern, length, date, optional mood rating)
- Your streak and practice statistics
- Your preferences: appearance, breathing motion, circle colour, ambient sound,
  custom rhythm, and reminder time

This data lives in the app's private storage. Deleting Breatho removes all of it
permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Breatho schedules a local notification on your
device. Local notifications are handled entirely by iOS. No notification content
is sent through us or any third party.

## Purchases

Breatho Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's In-App
Purchase system. Payment, billing, and renewal are handled by Apple — we never
see your payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription, Breatho reads your purchase
receipt through Apple's StoreKit framework. This happens on your device and tells
us only whether the subscription is active. Apple's own privacy policy governs
how Apple handles your purchase data.

## Third-party services

Breatho contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no
social media integrations. There are no third parties receiving your data,
because there is no data being sent anywhere.

## Health data

Breatho does not read from or write to Apple Health.

## Children

Breatho does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children
under 13. Since no information is collected at all, there is nothing to delete.

## Your rights

Because all your data is stored only on your device, you already have complete
control over it:

- **Access it** — everything is visible in the app's Progress screen
- **Delete it** — remove the app, or delete Breatho's data in iOS Settings
- **Export it** — your session history is included in an encrypted iPhone backup
  if you have backups enabled

There is no request you need to send us, because we hold nothing.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a
new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release
notes.
