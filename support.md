## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Breatho has no sign-up and no login. Everything works offline and all of
your data stays on your iPhone.

### What's included for free?

Eight breathing patterns, sessions up to 5 minutes, the check-in calendar,
the streak counter, and the daily reminder — no payment required.

### What does Breatho Pro include?

- All 14 breathing patterns
- 10, 15, and 20 minute sessions
- All six ambient sounds
- Custom rhythms you set yourself
- All breathing guide styles
- Practice insights and full session history

### How much does Pro cost?

- **Yearly — $19.99 per year**, including a 7-day free trial for new subscribers
- **Monthly — $4.99 per month**

Prices in other countries are set by Apple in your local currency. Payment is
charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew
automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current
period.

### How do I cancel?

Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap
**Subscriptions** and select Breatho. You can cancel there at any time.
Cancelling during the free trial means you are not charged.

### How do I switch between monthly and yearly?

In Breatho, go to **Settings › Subscription › Change plan**. Switching from
monthly to yearly takes effect immediately, and Apple refunds the unused portion
of your current period.

### I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open **Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases** in Breatho, using the same
Apple Account you purchased with. If that doesn't work, email us.

### Can I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with
your Apple Account.

### Does Breatho collect my data?

No. Breatho collects nothing — there is no account, no analytics, and no
advertising. See our [Privacy Policy](https://kax168.github.io/breatho/).

### Is Breatho a medical app?

No. Breatho is a guided breathing timer. It is not a medical device and is not
intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you have a
health concern, please talk to a doctor.
